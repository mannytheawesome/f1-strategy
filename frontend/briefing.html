F1 Strategy Predictor is an independent project — not affiliated with Formula 1, the FIA, or any team.

Predictions come from a lap-by-lap race simulation backtested against real results from the 2023–2026 seasons, with probabilities calibrated to avoid false confidence rather than chase a flattering hit-rate — accuracy sharpens once real laps exist, and a pre-race projection built from practice/qualifying data alone is shown as a range, not a guarantee. Not betting advice. Timing, lap, and result data via the OpenF1 API.
